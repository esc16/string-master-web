2. (Health & Workout Data - Apple HealthKit & Google Health Connect) With your explicit permission, the app reads tennis workout records (date, start/end time, and duration) from Apple HealthKit (iOS) or Google Health Connect (Android) solely to help you log tennis playtime to your rackets and track string lifespan. 3. (Data Analysis & Posting) The developer may analyze anonymized equipment and string data for statistical purposes and share reports/insights on blogs, social media, or Reddit. No personally identifiable information (PII) or personal health data is ever included.
