How to Use String Master Pro

A quick visual reference showing exactly where in the UI and how to use all premium features.

⌚ Smartwatch Sync ⚡ String Presets 📊 My Reports 📁 CSV Export 🎾 3+ Rackets & Specs 📱 Home Screen Widget
⌚
Smartwatch Workout Auto-Sync
📍 Where to Find It: Watch Workout App ➔ Open String Master (Pop-up Sheet)
  1. Track Your Match on Your Watch: On your Apple Watch (Workout app) or Galaxy Watch (Samsung Health), start your match using the 'Tennis' workout mode.
  2. Open String Master: After playing, simply open the String Master app on your phone.
  3. One-Tap Detection: The app automatically checks for recent tennis sessions from the last 3 days that you haven't logged yet. A 'Tennis Workout Detected! 🎾' card slides up right away.
  4. Save in 1 Tap: Your main racket is already selected. Adjust the time with +0.5h or -0.5h if needed, then tap [Log to This Racket]!
💡 Quick Tip: Be sure to choose the 'Tennis' workout on your watch. Workouts saved as 'Other' or 'HIIT' are skipped so your string hours stay accurate.
⚡
Favorite String Presets
📍 Where to Find It: Profile Tab ➔ String Presets  |  Log Screen ➔ [Load Preset] / [Save Setup]
  1. Save Your Go-To Setup: Go to Profile ➔ String Presets and tap [Add Preset], or tap [Save Setup] right inside the string logging screen after choosing your strings, gauges, and tensions. Give it a memorable name like "Match Poly 52 lbs".
  2. Apply in 1 Second: Next time you restring any racket, tap [Load Preset] at the top of the string logging screen.
  3. Zero Re-Typing: Pick your saved preset and every string, gauge, and tension fills in automatically!
💡 Quick Tip: Create different presets for winter (softer tension) and summer (firmer tension) and apply them to any racket in your bag.
📊
Smart Weekly & Monthly Reports
📍 Where to Find It: Profile Tab ➔ My Reports
  1. Delivered Automatically: Every Monday at 9:00 AM (your local time), you get a Push Notification & Email summarizing your past week on court.
  2. Check Anytime in the App: Tap the Profile tab at the bottom, then choose 'My Reports'.
  3. See Your Court Trends: Switch between Weekly and Monthly views to see your total hours played, how your string tension drops over time for each racket, and when it's time to restring.
💡 Quick Tip: Monthly reports also arrive on the 1st of every month for an easy-to-read summary of your court time and string wear.
📁
Full History CSV Data Export
📍 Where to Find It: History Tab ➔ Top-Right [Export CSV] Icon
  1. Open History: Tap the History tab in the bottom navigation bar.
  2. Tap Export CSV: Tap the share/export icon in the top-right corner to open Export Data to CSV.
  3. Pick What You Need: Choose from 3 options:
    • Stringing Logs: Rackets, strings, gauges, tensions, hours played, ratings & reviews.
    • Racket Playtime History: Dates, durations, court feel, weather, temperature & notes.
    • Export Everything (Both CSVs): Share both your stringing logs and playtime history together.
  4. Open Anywhere: Your phone's share menu opens right away—send it via AirDrop, save to Files/Drive, or open it cleanly in Microsoft Excel, Apple Numbers, or Google Sheets with no garbled characters.
🎾
3+ Rackets & Custom Specs
📍 Where to Find It: Home Tab (+ Button)  |  Profile Tab ➔ My Rackets
  1. Keep Your Whole Bag Ready: Free accounts can log unlimited jobs and playtime for 2 active rackets. Pro members can track 3+ rackets simultaneously with no limits.
  2. Save Pro Tuning Specs: When adding or editing a racket, expand 'Advanced Racket Specs' to record your exact customization:
    • Weight (grams) & Balance Point (mm)
    • Swingweight & Frame Stiffness (RA)
    • Serial Number & Lead Tape / Grip Setup Notes
📱
Home Screen Widgets
📍 Where to Find It: Profile Tab ➔ Home Screen Widget
  1. Pick Your Widget Size (Android): Tap Profile ➔ Home Screen Widget and choose between the Small Widget (2x2) for your string health gauge & tension, or the Medium Widget (4x2) with specs, playtime, and a quick-log button.
  2. Add to Your Screen (iOS): Touch and hold an empty space on your iPhone Home Screen, tap '+' at the top left, and search for 'String Master'.
💡 Quick Tip: Your widget updates automatically whenever you log playtime or restring your primary racket! Pro members can also tap the '+' / 'Log Playtime' button right inside the widget for 1-tap logging.
