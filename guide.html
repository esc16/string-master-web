How to Use String Master Pro

A quick visual reference showing exactly where in the UI and how to use all premium features.

⌚ Smartwatch Sync ⚡ String Presets 📊 Weekly & Monthly Reports 📁 CSV History Export 🎾 3+ Rackets & Pro Specs
⌚
Smartwatch Workout Auto-Sync
📍 UI Location: Smartwatch Workout App ➔ String Master Launch Screen (Auto-Prompt Bottom Sheet)
  1. Record Workout on Your Watch: On your Apple Watch (Workout app) or Galaxy Watch (Samsung Health), start an exercise and select the 'Tennis' workout mode.
  2. Open String Master: After your match, open the String Master mobile app on your iPhone or Android phone.
  3. Auto-Detection Prompt: The app automatically inspects unprocessed tennis workouts from the last 3 days. A 'Tennis Workout Detected' bottom sheet immediately slides up on your screen.
  4. Confirm or Fine-Tune: Your primary racket is pre-selected. Tap `+0.5h` or `-0.5h` if you need to adjust duration, then tap [Log Playtime] to save in 1 tap!
💡 Important Pro Tip: Make sure you record your workout under the official 'Tennis' category on your watch. Workouts recorded as 'Other' or 'HIIT' are intentionally skipped to prevent accidental playtime additions.
⚡
Favorite String Presets
📍 UI Location: Home Screen ➔ Tap Racket ➔ [+ Log Job] ➔ Top Preset Button
  1. Saving a New Preset: When logging a new string job on the Log Job Screen, select your Main & Cross strings (single or hybrid), gauges, and tensions. Before saving, check the toggle 'Save as Preset' and give it a nickname (e.g., "Tour Match Setup - 52 lbs").
  2. Applying a Preset in 1 Second: The next time you restring any racket, tap [+ Log Job] and press the [⭐ Presets] button at the very top.
  3. Instant Auto-Fill: Tap your desired preset from the list. All string models, gauges, and tensions populate instantly across your setup with zero typing required!
💡 Pro Tip: You can store multiple presets for different seasons (e.g., winter soft poly vs. summer crisp hybrid) and apply them to any racket in your bag.
📊
Smart Weekly & Monthly Reports
📍 UI Location: Weekly Push Notification ➔ Profile Tab ➔ 'Reports & Analytics'
  1. Automated Delivery: Every Monday at 9:00 AM (your local time), active players receive a localized Push & Email notification with their weekly court summary.
  2. Viewing Historical Reports: Open String Master and tap the Profile (👤) tab in the bottom navigation bar, then select 'Weekly & Monthly Reports'.
  3. In-Depth Insights: Toggle between Weekly and Monthly tabs to review total hours on court, session frequencies, tension health decay curves per racket, and recommended restringing schedules.
💡 Pro Tip: Monthly reports arrive on the 1st of every month, providing a high-level retrospective on gear wear and tear.
📁
Full History CSV Data Export
📍 UI Location: Logs Tab (📝) ➔ Top-Right [Export / Share] Icon
  1. Navigate to Logs: Tap the Logs (📝) tab at the bottom of the screen.
  2. Tap Export: Press the CSV Export icon located in the top-right corner of the app bar.
  3. Choose Export Scope: Select between:
    • Stringing Jobs: Date strung, tension, main/cross brands, gauges, and stringer notes.
    • Playtime Sessions: Match dates, durations, playability feel, weather, temperature (°C/°F), and session notes.
    • Full Archive: Exports both tables simultaneously.
  4. Native Share: The native iOS / Android share sheet opens instantly. Send the files to your computer via AirDrop, save to Google Drive/Files, or open directly in Microsoft Excel, Apple Numbers, or Google Sheets (encoded in UTF-8 with BOM for zero font corruption).
🎾
3+ Rackets & Tour Tuning Specs
📍 UI Location: Home Tab ➔ Swipe Racket Carousel ➔ [+ Add Racket]
  1. Managing 3+ Rackets: Free tier players can manage up to 2 rackets forever. Pro members can add unlimited rackets to their active bag.
  2. Advanced Tuning Specs: When adding or editing a racket, tap 'Advanced Racket Specs' to track your custom specs:
    • Static Weight (grams)
    • Swingweight (kg·cm²)
    • Balance Point (mm / pts HL)
    • Frame Stiffness (RA)
    • Custom Lead Tape & Grip customization notes
